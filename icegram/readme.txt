== Installation ==

= Option 1: Install Directly from Your WordPress Dashboard (Recommended) =

1. Log in to your WordPress Admin Dashboard and navigate to **Plugins > Add New**.
2. Search for **Icegram Engage**.
3. Click **Install Now** next to *Icegram Engage – Popups, Welcome Bars, Optins, Slide-Ins & Lead Generation for WordPress*, then click **Activate**.
4. Click **Icegram Engage** in your WordPress admin sidebar to customize your first ready-made campaign or import a design from the Template Gallery.

= Option 2: Manual ZIP Upload =

1. Download the `icegram.zip` archive from the [WordPress.org plugin page](https://wordpress.org/plugins/icegram/).
2. In your WordPress Admin Dashboard, go to **Plugins > Add New > Upload Plugin**, select `icegram.zip`, click **Install Now**, and click **Activate Plugin**.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Are there any limits on the number of popups, campaigns, or pageviews in the free version? =

No. Unlike SaaS popup tools that cap your free account at 500–1,000 monthly views or freemium plugins that limit you to 3 popups, Icegram Engage lets you create **unlimited campaigns, popups, action bars, slide-ins, and toast notifications** with **unlimited impressions** and no forced branding.

= How does Icegram Engage compare to OptinMonster, Popup Maker, or Hustle? =

OptinMonster requires an external SaaS subscription after 500 impressions, Hustle caps free users at 3 popups and 3 slide-ins, and Popup Maker focuses solely on modal popups. Icegram Engage is 100% WordPress-native, offers 4 distinct message types in the free version (Popups, Action Bars/Hello Bars, Slide-In Messengers, and Toast Notifications) with unlimited campaigns, and unlocks 8 more formats plus Exit-Intent, A/B testing, and Analytics in [Icegram Engage Max](https://www.icegram.com/engage/pricing/).

= Will Icegram Engage slow down my website or break page caching? =

No. Icegram Engage is lightweight and includes a built-in **Lazy Load** setting (**Icegram Engage > Settings**) that loads campaign assets asynchronously after your page finishes rendering, ensuring fast page speeds and compatibility with WP Rocket, LiteSpeed Cache, W3 Total Cache, and Cloudflare.

= Will mobile popups hurt my Google SEO rankings? =

Icegram Engage includes mobile-responsive templates and device-specific targeting rules so you can show non-intrusive Action Bars, Slide-Ins, or timed messages on mobile devices while reserving full popups for desktop visitors—protecting your mobile UX and Google search rankings.

= How do I integrate opt-in forms or email marketing services into a popup? =

You can embed forms in three ways:
1. Select an [Icegram Express](https://wordpress.org/plugins/email-subscribers/) or [Icegram Collect](https://wordpress.org/plugins/icegram-rainmaker/) form directly inside the campaign editor.
2. Paste any shortcode from Contact Form 7, WPForms, Gravity Forms, Ninja Forms, or Forminator into the message body.
3. Click the **Embed Form** button above the message editor and paste raw HTML form code from Mailchimp, AWeber, ActiveCampaign, ConvertKit, or any ESP—Icegram Engage automatically cleans up the HTML to fit your popup theme.

= Can I use shortcodes inside an Icegram Engage message? =

Yes. Both message headlines and body content support WordPress shortcodes, allowing you to embed forms, WooCommerce product blocks, videos, or dynamic content inside any popup, action bar, or slide-in.

= Why does my campaign preview not show my latest edits when I refresh the browser? =

Refreshing the frontend browser tab directly may serve a cached preview. Always click the **Preview** button inside the Icegram Engage campaign editor screen to load your most recent unsaved changes.

= Why do my messages have extra line breaks or unusual formatting? =

Extra paragraphs are usually caused by blank lines in raw HTML or theme CSS conflicts. Switch the message editor to **Text/HTML** mode and remove blank lines between HTML tags. You can also add custom CSS directly in the message or your theme customizer.

= How do I prevent a popup from showing repeatedly to the same visitor? =

In your campaign's **Display Rules > Retargeting** section, configure cookie rules to hide the campaign for X days (or for the current session) once the message has been shown or once the visitor clicks the CTA / submits the form.

= How do I add custom CSS or JavaScript to a message? =

You can include `<style>` or `<script>` tags directly inside the message HTML editor (in Text mode), and Icegram Engage will preserve and execute them when that specific message renders.
